# CONTROLS

This version of minesweeper was made to play with keyboard only, not with mouse

## MENU

Moving through options: **WASD**/**ARROWS**

Accept: **ENTER**/**SPACE**/**Z**

Cancel: **ESC**

## IN-GAME

Move: **WASD**/**ARROWS**

Open tile: **ENTER**/**SPACE**/**Z**

Flag tile: **X**
